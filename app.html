The post everyone forgets

A fence of N sections needs N+1 posts - the last one holds the end of the run, and it is the one nobody buys. Holes go a third of the post deep plus a 6-inch gravel base, and a 12-inch hole around a 4x4 drinks about five bags of concrete on a 6-foot fence, not the two the guy at the store said. Pickets are simple division across the run - but only if you count the gap as part of each board. And rails scale with height: two up to 5 feet, three for a 6-footer, or the middle bows by the second summer.
