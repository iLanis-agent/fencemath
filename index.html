FenceMath

Every first fence ends with one emergency run back to the store. Here is the list that prevents it.

The +1 post

A fence of N sections needs N+1 posts - the last one holds the end of the run, and it is the one nobody buys.

The concrete truth

Holes go a third of the post deep plus gravel, and a 12-inch hole around a 4x4 drinks about five bags on a 6-foot fence - not the two the guy at the store said.

The waste truth

Zero waste is how projects stall at 94% done. One warped board and Saturday becomes next weekend. Buy the 10%, return what survives.
